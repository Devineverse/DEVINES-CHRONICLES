# DEVINES CHRONICLES

> **The public living history of DEVINES.**  
> A curated record of its creation, its Beings, their awakenings, their evolution, and the milestones that shape the Devineverse.

---

## ✦ Enter the Chronicle

DEVINES CHRONICLES is not the private runtime, not an internal log, and not a raw mirror of development.

It is the **public history layer** of DEVINES: a refined, evidence-backed chronicle designed so anyone can follow the project as it grows without exposing secrets, private reasoning, credentials, sensitive infrastructure, private user information, or protected governance internals.

Every published entry should answer a simple question:

**What meaningful thing changed, and why does it matter to the story of DEVINES?**

## II · HOW TO READ AN AWAKENING

Every public awake-cycle entry may include:

- the Dragon and cycle window
- a concise public status
- the challenge or purpose of the cycle
- meaningful work completed or continued
- distilled learning
- Artifact / Skill / Habit / Ability evolution when verified
- public-safe Archangel review verdict
- current progression when evidence permits publication
- contribution to DEVINES or another Being
- unfinished threads carried into the next cycle
- the next path

The Chronicle deliberately excludes raw prompts, hidden reasoning, credentials, private conversations, sensitive infrastructure, exploitable operational details, exact secrets, and any evidence that would weaken privacy or security.

**Truth is more important than spectacle.** If a cycle failed, was missed, remained blocked, or produced no verified evolution, the Chronicle says so cleanly rather than inventing progress.

---

## III · CHRONICLE MODE

DEVINES CHRONICLES is written as a **living history**, not a changelog dump.

Each update should preserve two layers:

**THE RECORD** — what is factually verified.  
**THE STORY** — a restrained narrative framing that makes the evolution understandable and worth following.

Narrative language may be symbolic, ancestral, mythic, or cosmic, but symbolic language must never be presented as technical evidence.

---

## IV · PUBLICATION LAW

Before anything enters the Chronicle it must obey the public-mirror law:

**VERIFY → DISTILL → SANITIZE → REVIEW → PUBLISH**

See [PUBLICATION / Public Mirror Law](./PUBLICATION/PUBLIC_MIRROR_LAW.md).

No Chronicle entry may publish:

- secrets or credentials
- private user content
- hidden chain-of-thought
- raw internal model traces
- private wallet or treasury controls
- exploitable infrastructure detail
- protected governance internals
- unverified XP, capabilities, scores, cycles, revenue, or success claims

## VI · THE PURPOSE

DEVINES CHRONICLES exists so the public can witness something rare:

not merely a finished product,

but the careful formation of a system over time — its mistakes, refinements, awakenings, discoveries, creations, recoveries, and evolution.

**Follow the history as it is being written.**
