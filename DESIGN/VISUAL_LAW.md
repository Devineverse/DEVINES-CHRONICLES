# DEVINES Visual Law

DEVINES CHRONICLES is presented as a living codex, not conventional developer documentation.

## Foundation

**Canonical experience: Dark.**

The site-wide palette comes only from the canonical AUM / Monad visual field:

- **Void Black** — `#000000`
- **Deep Monad** — `#12071F`
- **Monad Violet** — `#3B176B`
- **DEVINES Violet** — `#7C4DFF`
- **AUM Lavender** — `#C7B8FF`
- **AUM Light** — `#EEE9FF`
- **White** — `#FFFFFF`

Primary reading text is white or AUM Light on black.

No global gold, blue, red, green, teal, or other accent family is introduced by the site architecture. Those colors may exist **inside a Being's canonical portrait** because they belong to that Being; they do not become interface colors.

## Depth law

The deeper the reader moves into DEVINES, the more light may emerge from the Void:

**Black → Deep Monad → Monad Violet → DEVINES Violet → AUM Lavender → White**

This is not a page-by-page rainbow. It is a restrained depth system.

- Landing / threshold: mostly Void Black, AUM sigil, white.
- BOOK I: black with restrained Deep Monad.
- BOOK II: black with stronger Monad/DEVINES violet around identity thresholds.
- BOOK III: deeper violet/lavender may surface around durable artifacts.
- BOOK IV: return toward black, with white/violet used as evidence light.
- THE CALL: highest contrast — Void, AUM light, white.

Body areas remain predominantly black throughout.

## Typography

### Display / Book titles

Use an inscriptional, carved, rune-adjacent character without becoming novelty fantasy typography.

Preferred direction: **Cinzel** or the closest restrained inscriptional serif available in GitBook.

Use display typography for:

- DEVINES CHRONICLES
- BOOK I / II / III / IV
- Series names
- Being names
- THE CALL

### Body

Use a highly legible neutral sans-serif.

Preferred order:

1. General Sans
2. Inter
3. closest GitBook-native neutral sans

Body text remains white / soft-white on black.

## Being pages

Each Being page opens with:

1. canonical portrait
2. BEING NAME · ID
3. title / divinity
4. spirit aspects
5. first-person threshold
6. current public cycle
7. durable trace
8. decentralized anchor when public

The portrait is part of identity, not decoration.

## Interface restraint

Prefer:

- black surfaces
- white typography
- violet/lavender links and dividers
- very low corner radius
- restrained borders
- little or no shadow
- no colorful SaaS gradients
- no playful badges
- no emoji in the published Book UI
- no repeated membership CTAs

The membership threshold belongs at **THE CALL**.
