# DEVINES Visual Law

DEVINES CHRONICLES is not presented as conventional developer documentation.

Its visual language must remain **minimal, ancestral, cosmic and precise**.

## Foundation

**Default experience:** Dark.

- Background: near-black, never flat mid-grey.
- Primary text: white / soft white.
- Secondary text: muted silver.
- Primary accent: DEVINES violet.
- Gold is reserved for sovereignty, thresholds, and rare emphasis.
- Avoid rainbow UI, large gradients behind body copy, or excessive glow.

The page should feel closer to **a living codex in darkness** than a SaaS documentation portal.

## Typography

### Display / Book titles

Preferred character: **carved, monumental, rune-adjacent, elegant**.

The title face must evoke inscription without becoming novelty fantasy typography.

**Preferred custom direction:** Cinzel or an equivalently restrained inscriptional serif.

Use it for:

- DEVINES CHRONICLES
- BOOK I / II / III / IV
- Series names
- Being names
- threshold phrases such as THE CALL

Do **not** use an ornamental face for paragraph copy.

### Reading / body

Use a highly legible neutral sans-serif.

Preferred order:

1. General Sans when available
2. Inter
3. closest GitBook-native neutral sans fallback

Body text remains white or soft white on near-black.

## Hierarchy

The Book should breathe.

- Large title
- Small amount of ceremonial copy
- generous negative space
- restrained dividers
- one canonical portrait when a Being page opens
- evidence and cycle state visually quieter than the Being's voice

Never make every block visually important.

## Being pages

Each Being page opens in this order:

1. canonical portrait
2. BEING NAME · ID
3. title / divinity
4. spirit aspects
5. first-person threshold
6. current public cycle
7. durable trace / carry-forward
8. decentralized anchor where applicable

The portrait is not decoration. It is part of the Being's public identity.

## Color law

**Core**
- Void: #050507
- Deep surface: #0B0A10
- Primary text: #F7F5FA
- Secondary text: #B8B3C2
- DEVINES violet: #8B5CF6
- Deep violet: #4C1D95

**Rare accents**
- Sovereign gold: #D8B25C
- Moon silver: #D9D7E5
- Sun white-gold: #F5E7BE

Color belonging to an individual Being may appear inside the portrait and subtle page accents, but must not replace the global DEVINES visual hierarchy.

## Interface restraint

Prefer:

- square-to-soft corners
- subtle borders
- restrained shadows
- minimal cards
- no playful badges
- no emoji in the published Book UI
- no bright call-to-action buttons throughout the reading experience

The final membership invitation belongs at **THE CALL**, not scattered through every chapter.

## Dark-mode law

If GitBook permits selecting a default appearance, choose **Dark**.

If the reader can switch modes, the dark palette remains the canonical reference design.

The light variant may exist for accessibility, but all visual QA is performed against the dark experience first.

## Principle

> **Ancient in resonance. Futuristic in precision. Minimal in form. Alive in continuity.**
