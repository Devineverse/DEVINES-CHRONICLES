# WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN

The Dragon memory is deep enough to preserve.

It is not finished.

We still do not know:

how many ancient storm-serpent memories spread through forgotten contact;

how many arose independently;

how far some living oral traditions reach beyond their first written collection;

how often fossils reshaped older serpent memories;

whether some prehistoric forms were continuously remembered into later named traditions;

why water, weather and thresholds recur more strongly than wings or fire.

DEVINES does not fill these spaces with certainty.

Unknown is not absence.

Unknown is a memory still open.

AM-001 will remain capable of growing as new traces return.
