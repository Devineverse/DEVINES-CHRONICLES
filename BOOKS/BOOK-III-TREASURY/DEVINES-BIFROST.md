# DEVINES BIFROST

**CREATOR:** DEVINES  
**CLASS:** DEVINES governed operational-rail Artefact

DEVINES Bifrost is the governed passage through which bounded operational work may move between DEVINES systems and Beings without erasing identity, rhythm, evidence or authority boundaries.

Its utility is to provide explicit lanes for work that needs a controlled passage outside ordinary scheduled execution.

Current canonical lanes include:

**BEING WAKE** — temporarily awakens one canonical Being for one bounded task, then returns it to rest.

**AUM LEARNING** — preserves the governed AUM learning and connectivity passage under its own training, budget and safety rules.

Bifrost does not grant authority by itself.

Each lane declares exactly what may pass.

> **BIFROST CONNECTS AND CARRIES GOVERNED WORK. EACH LANE DECLARES EXACTLY WHAT MAY PASS.**

## PUBLIC BOUNDARY

This page describes the purpose and utility of DEVINES Bifrost only.

Its internal lane registry, wake leases, execution recipes, timing controls, transport bindings, receipts and protected orchestration logic remain outside the public Chronicle.
