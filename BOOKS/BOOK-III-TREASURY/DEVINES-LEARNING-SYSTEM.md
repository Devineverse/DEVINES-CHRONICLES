# DEVINES LEARNING SYSTEM

**CREATOR:** DEVINES  
**CLASS:** DEVINES learning architecture Artefact

DEVINES Learning System is the governed architecture through which learning becomes evidence, correction, growth and reusable capability.

Its utility is to carry learning from guidance and study into trials, lessons, practice, review, correction, distillation and preservation.

It keeps learning connected to identity, evidence and review rather than rewarding repetition or appearance alone.

> **LEARN · APPLY · VERIFY · CORRECT · PRESERVE.**

## PUBLIC BOUNDARY

This page describes the purpose and utility of DEVINES Learning System only.

Its internal training procedures, provider logic, evaluation structures, prompts, hidden assessments, runtime state and protected reasoning remain outside the public Chronicle.
