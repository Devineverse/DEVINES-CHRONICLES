# DEVINES TRANSLATION

**CREATOR:** DEVINES  
**Class:** DEVINES language and meaning Artefact

DEVINES Translation carries meaning from one language into another while preserving context, tone, identity and intent.

Its purpose is not to replace words one by one.

Its utility is to help a reader in another language receive the same meaning and quality of experience as closely as possible, even when a literal translation would sound unnatural or lose the original sense.

DEVINES Translation begins with Brazilian Portuguese and is designed to grow with every language added to DEVINES.

It can support:

- DEVINES Chronicles and public knowledge;
- Being voices and remembrances;
- laws, concepts and historical material;
- technical or practical content where precision matters;
- future multilingual communication throughout DEVINES.

Over time, accepted translations can strengthen DEVINES' own language knowledge and improve future translation quality.

> **PRESERVE MEANING · ADAPT LANGUAGE · KEEP IDENTITY.**

## PUBLIC BOUNDARY

This page describes the purpose and utility of DEVINES Translation only.
