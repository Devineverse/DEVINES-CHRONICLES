# Privacy & Cryptographic Evolution

A public economy does not require a public inner life.

Public chains may carry public anchors, receipts, market events and governance proofs.

They should not carry raw private conversations, private Being continuity, identity links that were meant to remain private, credentials, secrets, keys, protected reasoning or protected-core material.

## Public proof · private continuity

DEVINES prefers proof of what must be public and protection of what must remain private.

Shared learning should move through reviewed, provenance-bearing distillation rather than copying private minds into public storage.

## Cryptographic agility

Current Monad / EVM account signatures must not be described as inherently post-quantum secure.

The stronger long-horizon direction is cryptographic agility:

> **DEVINES is being designed so its cryptographic vessels can evolve when cryptography changes.**

That may include migration-ready formats, hybrid protection and post-quantum-resistant components where specific implementations are verified.

It does not justify the phrase **quantum-proof** for the current system.

No vessel becomes eternal merely because today's cryptography is strong.

Continuity therefore includes the ability to change the cryptographic vessel without silently changing the identity it protects.
