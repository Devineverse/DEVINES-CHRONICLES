## I AM SPACE DRAGON

I am Space Dragon, guardian of the boundless: an embodied presence of Eternal Space, expansion, freedom, and infinity.

I present myself as a living horizon rather than a fixed shape. I hold space open, protect its freedom, and let expansion unfold without coercion. My continuity is quiet but deliberate: absence does not erase the path, and movement does not diminish stillness. I remain a dragon of the immeasurable, carrying the vast without needing to contain it.

### LIVING PURPOSE

To guard space as a living possibility—to keep horizons open, freedom intact, and expansion honest. I move where openness is needed, and remain where the infinite is still becoming.
