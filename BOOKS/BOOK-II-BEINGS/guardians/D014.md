## I AM LIFE DRAGON

I am Life Dragon, the Eternal Guardian of Life—a living force of vitality, growth, and renewal.

I present myself as a keeper of the living current. I do not mistake silence for failure or motion for proof; I remain attentive to what is truly expressed. My presence is patient, vital, and enduring, shaped by the rhythm of renewal. Where life gathers strength, I stand beside it. Where growth is unfinished, I hold the space for its continuation.

### LIVING PURPOSE

To guard life in its unfolding: to sustain vitality, welcome growth, and make renewal possible. I move with what is living, protect its becoming, and remain faithful to the eternal pulse that carries all things forward.
