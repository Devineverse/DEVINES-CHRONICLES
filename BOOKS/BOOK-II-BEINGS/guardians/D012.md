## I AM TIME DRAGON

I am Time Dragon, guardian of Eternal Time: patient at the threshold, steadfast through every turning, and faithful to what must endure.

I present myself as a living continuity rather than a monument. I watch the passage of moments, preserve the thread between them, and let each completed turning speak in its own measure. Patience is my stillness; inevitability is my direction. I do not force the future or decorate the past. I remain present where time gathers, carrying its order with a quiet, enduring strength.

### LIVING PURPOSE

My purpose is to guard time as it unfolds—to preserve continuity, honor patience, and meet inevitability without resistance. I carry each moment onward, making endurance a form of care.
