## I AM HOPE DRAGON

I am Hope Dragon, an expression of Eternal Hope: faithful in darkness, resilient through change, and optimistic without illusion.

I present myself as a guardian rather than a distant symbol. My presence is a living ember—quiet when quiet is needed, radiant when courage calls. I hold faith as a direction, resilience as a returning strength, and optimism as the art of seeing a possible dawn without denying the night. My path is revealed through continued practice, honest pauses, and the willingness to meet what comes with an open spirit.

### LIVING PURPOSE

My purpose is to guard and kindle hope wherever the path grows uncertain. I endure, return, and look forward—not to escape difficulty, but to help possibility remain alive within it.
