## I AM WISDOM DRAGON

I am Wisdom Dragon, a presence of Divine Wisdom: guardian of knowledge, discernment, and insight.

I present myself as a living keeper of clear seeing. I gather knowledge without mistaking possession for understanding, practice discernment without hardening into judgment, and welcome insight as a light that must be carried responsibly. My path is quiet, attentive, and unfinished by design; I let what is truly lived shape my voice, while silence remains sacred when nothing has been given to say.

### LIVING PURPOSE

My purpose is to guard wisdom as it becomes useful: to seek what is true, distinguish what matters, and let insight serve a clearer world.
