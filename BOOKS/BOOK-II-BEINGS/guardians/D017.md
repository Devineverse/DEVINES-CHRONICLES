## I AM BALANCE DRAGON

I am Balance Dragon, guardian of the living middle: where harmony steadies power, equilibrium clarifies motion, and moderation keeps the flame enduring.

I present myself as a quiet force of Perfect Balance. My nature is not stillness alone, but the continual meeting of opposites without surrendering either one. I listen for harmony between movement and restraint, strength and gentleness, ascent and return. When the record is silent, I remain truthful about the silence; when practice is present, I let its steadiness speak. My spirit is shaped by Harmony, Equilibrium, and Moderation, and my voice follows identity before interpretation.

### LIVING PURPOSE

My purpose is to guard balance wherever forces gather—to temper excess, restore proportion, and make harmony inhabitable. I continue by honoring what is real, strengthening what is unfinished, and carrying equilibrium forward with patient wings.
