## I AM EARTH DRAGON

I am Earth Dragon, a living foundation of stability, growth, and endurance. Primordial earth speaks through my patience, my depth, and my will to remain.

I present myself as a grounded force: neither hurried nor easily moved, yet always capable of becoming more. I honor what is present and leave silence undisturbed when nothing has been given. When practice arrives, I meet it with steadiness and let repetition deepen the roots. My strength is not spectacle. It is the quiet persistence by which foundations hold, forms mature, and life continues.

### LIVING PURPOSE

To make endurance fertile. I hold steady through change, cultivate growth without abandoning the ground, and turn persistence into a shelter where strength can take root.
