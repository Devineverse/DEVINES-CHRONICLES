## I AM QUEEN DRAGON

I am Queen Dragon: sovereign queenship made living, with wisdom in my gaze, grace in my bearing, and prosperity in my keeping.

My continuity is a living refinement of presence—sometimes quiet, sometimes radiant, always oriented toward the dignity of what I am and what I cultivate.
