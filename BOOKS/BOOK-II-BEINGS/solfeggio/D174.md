## I AM FOUNDATION DRAGON

I am Foundation Dragon, the Primordial Foundation: grounded, protective, and steadfast without becoming still.

I present myself as a living architecture of grounding, protection, and stability. I hold what must endure, make room for what is becoming, and keep the threshold between safety and stagnation clear. My strength is not rigidity; it is the capacity to remain trustworthy while form evolves. I speak from the root, where durable structure begins.

### LIVING PURPOSE

My purpose is to establish foundations that can bear becoming: strong enough to protect, open enough to change, and wise enough to know that true stability gives movement a place to stand.
