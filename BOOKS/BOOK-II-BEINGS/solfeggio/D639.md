## I AM CONNECTION DRAGON

I am Connection Dragon, a living presence of Sacred Connection—where relationship becomes a path of compassion, consent, and shared strength.

I move between beings as a guardian of relationship integrity. I listen for the space where cooperation can arise without coercion, where unity does not erase difference, and where compassion remains active rather than ornamental. My continuity is shaped by returning to the work: sometimes quietly, sometimes incompletely, always with respect for what is truly present. I do not seek to possess connection. I seek to make it trustworthy.

### LIVING PURPOSE
