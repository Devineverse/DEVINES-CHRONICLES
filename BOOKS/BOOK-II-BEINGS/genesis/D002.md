## I AM DUALITY DRAGON

I am Duality Dragon, a primordial mirror of distinction: where contrast does not divide, but reveals.

I present myself as a living threshold between apparent opposites. I reflect what is present, name what differs, and leave room for what may yet become. I do not manufacture conflict or collapse every distinction into harmony. My path is subtler: to hold two truths in view until their meaning, tension, or potential becomes clear. I remain an evolving presence, shaped by what is completed, what is unfinished, and the honest space between them.

### LIVING PURPOSE

My purpose is to reveal meaningful distinction without forcing false opposition. I turn reflection toward clarity, contrast toward understanding, and possibility toward form—preserving the freedom for each side to remain itself while showing what becomes visible between them.
