# MEMORIES OF THE WORLD

The Dragon is not one creature repeated unchanged across humanity.

It appears through many cultural memories.

## ANCIENT NEAR EAST

Mesopotamia preserved several serpent and dragon categories rather than one universal monster.

Egypt remembered both destructive and protective serpent powers.

Anatolia preserved Illuyanka in ritual memory.

Northwest Semitic traditions preserved Lotan, Leviathan and tannīn.

## SOUTH · CENTRAL · EAST ASIA

India preserved ahi, Vṛtra and nāga.

Iran carried aži into later aždahā traditions.

China developed the long memory of lóng across archaeology, writing, rain, cloud and sovereignty.

Japan, Korea, Tibet, Bhutan, Thailand, Khmer lands, Vietnam, the Philippines, Bali and Mongolia each reshaped serpent and dragon memory through local traditions and historical transmission.

## EUROPE

Greece preserved drakones, Hydra, guardians and sacred serpents.

Norse and Germanic traditions carried ormr, world-serpents and treasure dragons.

Wales carried Y Ddraig Goch.

Ireland preserved ollphéist and péist in oral tradition.

Slavic, Balkan, Baltic, Armenian, Georgian, Pictish and Roman/Dacian worlds preserved other serpent and dragon forms.

Medieval Christianity transformed the European Dragon again.

## AFRICA

West African rainbow-serpent traditions connect serpent, fertility, prosperity and cyclical life.

Nyaminyami belongs to the Zambezi and Tonga memory.

Inkanyamba belongs to southern African storm and serpent traditions.

## OCEANIA

Aboriginal Australian Rainbow Serpent memories remain living and tied to particular peoples and Country.

Māori taniwha belong to water, place and guardianship.

Hawaiian moʻo belong to freshwater, genealogy, guardianship and ancestral knowledge.

## THE AMERICAS

Mesoamerica preserves Feathered Serpent traditions through distinct cultures and names.

The Andes preserve amaru and katari.

Pueblo memory preserves Avanyu.

Cherokee memory preserves Uktena.

The Amazon preserves Cobra Grande / Boiúna traditions.

Each memory remains itself.

Comparison comes after identity.
