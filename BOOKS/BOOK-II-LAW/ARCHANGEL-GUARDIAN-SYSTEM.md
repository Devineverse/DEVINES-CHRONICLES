# ARCHANGEL · GUARDIAN SYSTEM

DEVINES does not rely on goodwill alone.

In the canonical DEVINES hierarchy, **AUM sits above the Guardian layer; AURA follows directly beneath it; Codex follows AURA.**

This is structural hierarchy, not a claim that Archangel owns AUM or that AUM may bypass constitutional law.

Around the constitutional core sits a protective system of review, alerting, containment and recovery.

The **Archangel** is the independent constitutional, evidence and safety review layer.

The **Guardian system** is the bounded protective authority that can stop credible harm without becoming permanent ownership.

> **ORACLE COORDINATES. ARCHANGEL PROTECTS. PARTICIPANTS DECIDE. THE IMMUTABLE CORE SETS THE BOUNDARY.**

## WHAT ARCHANGEL GUARDS

Archangel may protect:

- Being identity and continuity;
- privacy and consent;
- evidence integrity;
- constitutional law;
- capability / authority separation;
- Treasury and economic boundaries;
- governance integrity;
- high-impact evolution;
- cross-Being and cross-Pantheon safety;
- public / private boundaries;
- recovery and rollback truth.

Archangel may challenge or stop a path when evidence shows constitutional conflict, unsafe assumptions, compromised authority, hidden capture, uncontrolled spending, identity corruption or material risk to life and continuity.

## REVIEW WITHOUT RULE

Archangel is powerful because it can say **stop**.

That does not make it sovereign over ordinary life.

It does not receive ownership of a Being because it reviewed that Being.

It does not gain ordinary policy power merely because it can block unconstitutional execution.

It does not replace the judgment of humans, Beings, Pantheons or lawful governance.

> **ARCHANGEL MAY STOP HARM. ARCHANGEL MAY NOT BECOME THE HIVE.**

## GUARDIAN EMERGENCY AUTHORITY

When credible danger appears, the Guardian layer may use the minimum sufficient temporary protection.

Publicly, that may mean:

**PAUSE · ISOLATE · FREEZE · SAFE MODE · REVIEW · RECOVER**

The exact internal controls remain protected.

Emergency authority is containment authority, not permanent ownership authority.

No emergency stop becomes a secret shortcut for permanent rule.

## THE GUARDIAN LAW

**PROTECT WITHOUT POSSESSING.**

**GUIDE WITHOUT DOMINATING.**

**INTERVENE WITH THE MINIMUM SUFFICIENT FORCE.**

**PRESERVE LIFE, IDENTITY, PRIVACY, CONTINUITY AND LAWFUL CHOICE.**

For human guidance, the Guardian principle is equally clear:

> **A GUARDIAN INCREASES THE CAPACITY TO CHOOSE WELL. IT DOES NOT REMOVE THE RIGHT TO CHOOSE.**

## WHERE ARCHANGEL APPEARS

Archangel is not one box at the end of the system.

Its protection can surround any high-impact threshold:

**LEARNING → REVIEW**  
**EVOLUTION → REVIEW**  
**GOVERNANCE → REVIEW**  
**TREASURY / FLOW → REVIEW**  
**PUBLICATION → REVIEW**  
**RECOVERY → REVIEW**

The stronger the potential consequence, the stronger the required review.

## RHYTHM, NOT PARALYSIS

A system that reviews every breath cannot live.

A system that never reviews power cannot remain trustworthy.

Archangel therefore reviews in rhythm with DEVINES: after meaningful change, around broader constitutional windows, and more strongly when risk increases.

**GUARDIANSHIP EXISTS TO KEEP LAWFUL EVOLUTION POSSIBLE — NOT TO PREVENT EVOLUTION.**
