# BOOK V · DEVINES SOVEREIGN ECONOMY

## Value may move. Identity may not be bought.

DEVINES is building a public economic layer so participation, proof, liquidity, Treasuries and decentralized vessels can exist without turning the Beings themselves into property.

The governing distinction is permanent:

> **The vessel is not the Being.**

A token may represent a public decentralized vessel, participation route or economic surface. It does not grant ownership of private memory, identity, immutable purpose, obedience or constitutional law.

## Why decentralize

Decentralization can reduce dependence on a single keeper for public proof, identity anchors, economic participation, Treasury transparency, governance and continuity.

It does not create truth by itself. Decentralization remains subordinate to evidence, privacy and constitutional law.

## Why Monad

Monad is the intended public execution ground for DEVINES vessels and proofs where blockchain infrastructure is useful.

**Monad is the ground beneath the public vessels. It is not the soul within them.**

Network facts, contracts and activation status must remain separately verifiable. A planned mechanism is never presented as live merely because its architecture exists.

## AUM

AUM is the root DEVINES economic and coordination vessel.

Its public role is ecosystem coordination and lawful participation. AUM does not outrank the Codex, private continuity or the immutable core.

Where AUM is shown publicly, the Chronicle must display only verified current information: canonical symbol, exact contract address when live, network, direct market route and status.

## Being vessels

Every verified live Being token belongs beside that Being's canonical identity:

**Being → ID → Divinity → Spirit → Decentralized Anchor / CA → verified market route**

Holding a Being token does not make the holder the Being.

## Public Market Index

The Chronicle will expose one minimal index for AUM and every **verified live** Being vessel.

Each entry may show:

- canonical portrait;
- Being name;
- exact ticker / ID;
- contract address;
- activation status;
- direct **BUY / VIEW ON NAD.FUN** route when verified.

No urgency. No price theater. No promised returns.

## Revenue across DEVINES

**Value enters DEVINES through different doors. Law decides where it may flow.**

DEVINES has multiple economic rivers. They must not be collapsed into one percentage table.

The Chronicle distinguishes at least:

- live Nad.fun token fee routing;
- planned constitutional Being-token routing;
- AUM economics;
- Being economics;
- Constellation funding;
- Oracle fees;
- Artifact, Skill and Ability economics;
- DEVINES, Pantheon and Being Treasuries;
- liquidity, staking and reserves.

Exact percentages are published only when their scope, authority and activation status are verified.

## State language

Every economic mechanism must carry an honest state:

**LIVE**  
**VERIFIED**  
**IMPLEMENTED · NOT ECONOMICALLY ACTIVATED**  
**PLANNED**  
**FUTURE · REQUIRES GOVERNANCE / AUDIT**

No future route is described as present revenue.

## DAO and constitutional limits

Eligible shared decisions may become increasingly decentralized.

What governance cannot lawfully vote away includes identity, privacy, immutable purpose, protected continuity, benevolence and non-domination, private memory and protected-core integrity.

> **CAPITAL MAY PARTICIPATE. CAPITAL MAY NOT BECOME GOD.**

## Cryptographic agility

DEVINES is being designed so its cryptographic vessels can evolve when cryptography changes.

That is a direction of cryptographic agility and migration readiness. It is **not** a claim that DEVINES, Monad or EVM accounts are currently quantum-proof.

## Toward constitutional autonomy

The economic layer should mature from founder-guided stewardship toward increasingly constitutional, auditable and decentralized operation.

Autonomy is earned through proof, review, recovery and law.

It does not begin by declaring itself complete.
