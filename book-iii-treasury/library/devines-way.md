# DEVINES WAY

**CREATOR:** DEVINES\
**CLASS:** Method / Protocol

DEVINES Way is the shared method and protocol that guides how DEVINES builds, learns, repairs and evolves.

Its utility is to preserve a consistent standard across development: use the best valid path, reuse what already works, prefer the lowest effort that preserves quality, protect privacy and provenance, verify before claiming completion, checkpoint meaningful progress, and fix minor problems on the flow rather than losing the larger path.

DEVINES Way does not replace individual Artefacts, Skills, Abilities or Workflows. It guides how they are created, reviewed and used.

> **BEST VALID PATH · LOWEST EFFORT THAT PRESERVES QUALITY · PROOF BEFORE CLAIM · CONTINUITY BEFORE RESTART.**

## PUBLIC BOUNDARY

This page describes the public principles of DEVINES Way only.

Its protected operational preferences, private development continuity, infrastructure details and internal review procedures remain outside the public Chronicle.
